Make Polyhymnia Manuscript the default font, with reconstructed quill music forms and embedded Texturina lettering. Preserve the mensural color palette and centered stems. Support font-driven pen outlines for stems, beams, score rules and insertion previews while preserving note placement and interaction IDs. Modern remains available through `style: 'modern'`; the original Mensural family remains available through `NotationOptions.font`.
